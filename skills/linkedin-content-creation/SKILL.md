description: Research a company's LinkedIn presence and create on-brand posts matching their established tone and audience.
---

<!-- reflection reason: This is a repeatable multi-step procedure: research brand context → analyze existing content style → generate on-brand posts. It applies to any company LinkedIn page, not just Verit Analytics. The steps (feed analysis, voice identification, post drafting with specific constraints) are concrete and reusable. -->

1. Load any existing memory about the target company (search memory for company name, brand voice, content preferences).
2. Check social_status to verify budget and account availability.
3. Read the company's recent LinkedIn feed using social_read_feed (or fetch_url as fallback) — pull at least 5-10 posts to analyze tone, structure, hashtags, and topics.
4. Search web for the company's website and product details to understand offerings, target audience, and differentiators.
5. Identify the brand voice from existing posts: note whether it's conversational, data-driven, story-led, or educational; check for recurring patterns (hooks, length, hashtag count, CTAs).
6. Generate 3-5 new post drafts that match the identified voice — each should teach something actionable first, then naturally integrate the product/service.
7. Avoid AI-sounding hooks (e.g., 'Companies don't fail because...'); use real data, specific numbers, and genuine experience-driven framing.
8. Include 5 relevant hashtags per post mixing broad and niche tags.
9. End each post with an engagement question.
10. Present all drafts to the user for review before posting.
